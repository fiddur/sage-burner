This app is run by the people organising the gathering you are part of. Using it is an agreement
with them and with nobody else — not with whoever wrote the software, who host nothing and can
see nothing, and not with any company, because there is not one.

Whatever you have agreed with those organisers about the gathering itself — what it costs, who
is admitted, how people are expected to be with each other while they are there — stands on its
own. This app is where some of that gets written down and kept track of. It is not a party to
it.

# Getting an account

Accounts here are given rather than opened. Either an organiser invites you, or you apply and
they decide; either way the invitation that reaches you carries a link that works once and then
expires. Signing in with Discord or Facebook is an extra way into an account you already have,
and never makes one.

Keep your way in to yourself. If you think somebody else is signed in as you, tell an
organiser — setting a new password is the one thing you cannot do for yourself.

# What you agree to

- **Be who you say you are.** The whole point of a membership list is that the people on it are
  the people coming.
- **What you write here, the other members read** — dreams you offer, comments, questions,
  notes, and the pictures you add to them. Write it for them.
- **What you read here about other people is theirs.** Contact details, allergies, who is
  sleeping where: it is in front of you so a gathering can be planned, and taking it somewhere
  else is not yours to do.
- **Put nobody else's details in.** Your list of ways to be reached is your own.
- Do not go looking for parts of the app that are not yours, and do not break it for anybody
  else.

# What the organisers can do

They run the gathering, so they decide its shape: the dates, who is admitted, what is recorded
as paid. They can take an account off, and they can stop running the installation altogether —
it is their server. If something they do here seems wrong to you, they are the people to take it
up with.

One thing the app will not do for them: it refuses to delete a member who has ever said they
were coming to a gathering, because that would take the record of the gathering with them. The
privacy policy says what that means in practice, under **How long it is kept, and getting rid of
it**.

# The software

sage-burner is free software under the AGPL, and its source is public. Anybody may run a copy of
it; this is somebody's copy.

**It comes with no warranty of any kind.** Nobody promises that it will be available, that a
notification will arrive, or that nothing will be lost — that is the licence's own text, and it
means what it says. Whoever runs this installation is the only one who could restore a backup,
and whether there is one is a question for them.

# Stopping

Stop using it whenever you like. Your picture, your ways of being reached and any provider you
have linked are all yours to take off under **Your details**. Removing an account entirely is
something to ask an organiser for, and the privacy policy says why there is no button for it.

# Who to ask

Whoever runs this gathering. They invited you, they hold what is stored, and the agreement is
with them.
